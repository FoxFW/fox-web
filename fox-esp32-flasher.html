Board not showing up or won't connect?
  • Flipper Wi-Fi Dev Board (ESP32-S2): hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then click Connect.
  • Other ESP32 boards: if Connect fails, hold the board's BOOT button while you click Connect.
  • Use a USB data cable plugged straight into the computer. Charge-only cables won't work.
  • Close anything else using the port: Arduino IDE, serial monitors, qFlipper, other flasher tabs.
  • You don't have to pick a model. The chip that answers decides which firmware is used, whatever is selected.
