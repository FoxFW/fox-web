Hardware

📊Connect an ESP32 to see which optional hardware (RFID/SubGHz/IR/GPS) is enabled.
WiFi
Scan, wardrive, sniff, and (once armed) attack.
BLE
General BLE bridge, tag detection (FINDMY/SMARTTAG/TILE/FLOCK), and (once armed) spam.
GPS
Fix/track readout and POI marking (optional hardware).
IR
Send, receive, and TV-B-Gone (optional hardware).
SubGHz
External CC1101-on-ESP32 radio control (optional hardware), independent of the Flipper's own radio.
RFID
PN532 breakout — scan/read/write Mifare (optional hardware).
AI
Gemini relay, cooldown-gated.
Settings
Fox_ESP32_FW settings, including the attacks-arm toggle.
